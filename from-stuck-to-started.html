Why this talk exists

Lots of ideas.
Hard to know where to begin.

As a child, I would become overwhelmed by projects and struggle to make progress. I often think about how AI could have helped me break those projects down.

This talk starts with that experience. I'll demonstrate how to get thoughts out, ask useful questions and turn a broad idea into a first action — with you making the decisions.

You don't need to master AI before it can be useful. We'll start with one everyday example.

An illustrative example

01
Get the thoughts out

“I want to organise a community event. I'm unsure about the venue, date and who to invite.”

02
Make the task smaller

Ask AI to clarify the goal, ask three questions and suggest a manageable starting point.

03
Start, check and choose

Draft a venue enquiry. Check the details and tone yourself before deciding what to do next.

Who is it for?

Adult community groups, libraries, volunteer teams and people who are curious about AI but unsure where to start.

For adult ADHD and autism groups, we'll discuss whether the session fits your members and agree the pace and access needs together. The talk draws on personal experience of overwhelm; it is not clinical advice.

No device or AI account is needed to watch the demonstration. Questions are welcome, and participation is optional.
