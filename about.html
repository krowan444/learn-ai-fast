Why I created Learn AI Fast

I know what it feels like to get stuck.

As a child, I would get overwhelmed by projects and struggle to make progress. I often think about how useful AI could have been for getting my thoughts out and breaking an idea into smaller steps.

That experience is behind Learn AI Fast and my signature talk, From Stuck to Started. I want to make AI approachable through useful tasks, patient explanations and honest discussion of its limits.

I’m building my AI education business and starting out as a speaker. My first group sessions are free pilots so I can gain experience and learn from feedback. Explore the talk.
