A few things you might wonder

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. We can start from the beginning. In a talk you can watch and ask questions; in a lesson we'll work through things together.

Are lessons and talks free?

One-to-one lessons are paid, with prices listed before booking. I'm offering a limited number of free introductory group talks while I build speaking experience. Ask about availability.

Where can we meet?

I'm based in Northampton. Group visits are generally within about an hour's drive, or we can meet online. Evenings and weekends are easiest; weekday daytime visits need arranging around work.

What if AI gets something wrong?

Checking is part of the learning. We'll discuss what information to keep private and when to verify an answer using another reliable source.
